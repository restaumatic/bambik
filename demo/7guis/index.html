7GUIs in Bambik

Implementation of 7GUIs tasks as Profunctor User Interfaces over six design-system vocabularies — Material Design Components 2 and 3, Shoelace, Fluent, Bootstrap, and plain HTML — written in Bambik. Followed up by nGUIs, the same treatment for popular web demo problems.

  1. Counter (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  2. Temperature Converter (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  3. Flight Booker (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  4. Timer (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  5. CRUD (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  6. Circle Drawer (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
  7. Cells (MDC2 | MDC3 | Shoelace | Fluent | Bootstrap | HTML)
